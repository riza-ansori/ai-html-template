01 Is there a free plan? +

Yes. The Free plan gives you access to the core workspace, basic AI generation, and a limited number of credits and projects.

02 Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +

Yes. You can cancel your paid subscription at any time. Your workspace remains available until the end of your current billing period.

03 What are AI credits? +

AI credits represent usage across Arc's generation features. Different AI actions may consume different amounts of credits depending on the complexity of the request.

04 Can I use Arc with my team? +

Yes. The Team plan provides a shared workspace, team analytics, shared templates, and additional collaboration features.

05 Is my data secure? +

Arc is designed with security and privacy in mind. For this template, security claims are demonstration content and should be replaced with the actual policies and infrastructure of the final product.
